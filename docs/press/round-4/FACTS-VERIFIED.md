## 1. Regulation 130A — Malaysia now has a kelulut-specific honey standard, and it is IN FORCE

Verified 2026-08-26. The English researcher flagged this but **could not fetch either primary
source** and correctly gated it. Confirmed independently.

- Instrument: **Food Regulations (Amendment No. 2) 2025**, issued **27 August 2025**.
- **In force 1 March 2026** — that is roughly six months ago, not a future change. Write it in the
  present tense.
- It inserts a **new Regulation 130A**, a product standard for **kelulut (stingless bee) honey**,
  and adds kelulut requirements to the **Fifteenth Schedule** (microbiological standards).
- It sets: **quality** limits (moisture, ash, and the sugars **trehalulose**, fructose, glucose and
  sucrose, plus HMF and pH); **safety** limits (the new microbiological requirements); and
  **labelling** — the package must state **"Kelulut Honey"**.
- Related Malaysian Standards published alongside it: **MS 2782:2025** Honey (Apis sp.) and
  **MS 2683:2025** dehydrated kelulut honey.

### Why this is the round's best material, and it is health-claim-free

Kelulut's dominant reducing sugar is **trehalulose**, not the fructose/glucose profile of *Apis*
honey. That is why stingless-bee honey does not fit the ordinary honey composition standard — the
old standard was written for a different insect. **Kelulut was not failing because it was fake; it
was being measured against a rule that was never about it.** Malaysia has now written the rule that
is.

That is a genuine, checkable, entirely non-medical explanation, and it **completes the story the
live `en/is-honey-in-malaysia-fake` tells**: that article already notes the 90%-fake claim
"explicitly included" kelulut and that this matters. The regulator has since agreed.

### Limits — read these before writing a word

- **SUBUR is not kelulut honey**, so Regulation 130A does **not** apply to it. Say so plainly.
  SUBUR is a ten-ingredient raw honeycomb blend; the honey standard relevant to ordinary honey is
  Regulation 130, already covered by the live English fact-check.
- **Do not publish a numeric threshold from 130A.** I verified the *categories* it regulates
  (moisture, ash, the four sugars, HMF, pH) and the labelling requirement, **not the numbers**. No
  writer may state a limit value until someone reads the gazetted P.U.(A) text itself.
- **Do not claim SUBUR complies with anything**, 130 or 130A. Nothing about SUBUR's testing is
  published, in either direction.
- Trehalulose may be named and explained as the distinguishing sugar. **No health, nutritional,
  glycaemic or "better for you" claim about it** — that is where the entire kelulut corpus goes and
  it is off limits here.

**Sources:** Tridge regulatory summary of the amendment; Food Compliance International, *New
Standard on Kelulut (Stingless bee) honey*; Department of Standards Malaysia announcement of MS
2683:2025; peer-reviewed work on trehalulose in stingless-bee honey (*Food Chemistry*,
S0308814621025723; *Scientific Reports* s41598-020-68940-0). All read 2026-08-26.

## 2. The KKM "Autentik" scheme covers honey, and the holders list IS public

Verified 2026-08-26 by **downloading and reading the actual PDF** — the Malay researcher explicitly
flagged that it had not opened it, and the widely repeated claim in Feb-2024 news was that the list
was *not* public.

- The scheme is **Skim Pensijilan Makanan Autentik**, run by **BKKM** (Bahagian Keselamatan dan
  Kualiti Makanan, Ministry of Health) at `https://hq.moh.gov.my/fsq/autentik`.
- The page publishes **"Senarai Pemegang Sijil Makanan Autentik Kementerian Kesihatan Malaysia"**,
  a downloadable PDF, **dated 23 July 2025**, 37 KB.
- I downloaded it. Its heading reads **"SENARAI PEMEGANG SIJIL AUTENTIK KKM — PRODUK MADU"**: it is
  specifically the **honey** holders list.
- Its columns are **BIL · NAMA SYARIKAT · ALAMAT · NO SIRI SIJIL · TARIKH TAMAT SIJIL** — company,
  address, certificate serial, **and an expiry date**.
- Holders visible in the file include Multi Food Manufacture Sdn Bhd (formerly Flora Bee), RCAB Food
  Ventures, Eminent Food Industries, B&B Town, MHP Miracle Health Products, QBees Marketing, Madu
  Pak Long ST, Eco Bee Biotech and SCC Marketing (M) Sdn Bhd. Certificate serials take the form
  `AU…` with a state letter.

### So the useful, checkable point is

**A shopper can verify an "Autentik" claim themselves**, against a named list with serial numbers
and expiry dates, published by the ministry. That is the Malaysian counterpart of the UMF-licence
logic the live manuka articles already use — and it is a far better answer than any home test.

### Limits

- **SUBUR is not on that list, and the site publishes no certification of any kind.** Do not imply
  otherwise, and do not imply that absence means a product is fake — the scheme is **voluntary**.
  The honest line is what the logo *does* prove when present, and how to check it.
- **Do not state the number of holders.** Feb-2024 reporting said seven; the July-2025 list is
  longer. Counting rows off a PDF whose extraction needed a custom decode is not a number worth
  publishing. Say the list is published and dated, and link it.
- Do not confuse Autentik with **MeSTI, GMP or HACCP**, which are different KKM schemes about
  safety and process rather than authenticity.

## 3. "Organic honey" — no Malaysian organic scheme covers honey

Verified 2026-08-26, backing the Arabic `العسل العضوي` article.

- Malaysia's organic certification scheme, **myOrganic**, covers **crops, livestock and
  aquaculture**. Its published scope does **not** include honey or beekeeping.
- **MS 1529** — the Malaysian organic standard — is **plant-based** production.
- For contrast, jurisdictions that *do* certify organic honey impose a forage-radius condition: the
  **EU requires roughly a 3 km radius** of compliant forage around the hive, because a beekeeper
  cannot control where bees fly. The **USDA NOP has no apiculture standard at all**.

**The article's honest thesis:** `organic` on a honey jar in Malaysia is not backed by a Malaysian
organic certification for honey, because none exists for it — exactly parallel to the live Arabic
raw-vs-filtered article's finding that **`خام` is not a regulated word**. Where a foreign organic
mark appears, the question is which scheme, and whether that scheme certifies apiculture at all.

**Limits:** name no specific brand; make no purity or health claim; do not say organic honey is
better or worse; and do not state SUBUR's status — it publishes no certification.

## 4. What did NOT verify, and must not be written

- **Any numeric limit inside Regulation 130A.** Categories confirmed, values not.
- **A count of Autentik honey holders.** See above.
- The Malay researcher's characterisation that the BKKM list "contradicts the claim that the list is
  secret" is **correct as of the 23 Jul 2025 file** — but the Feb-2024 reporting that the list was
  not public was also accurate at that time. Write it as *the list is published now*, not as *the
  reporting was wrong*.
