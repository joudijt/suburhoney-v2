Q: What makes Subur different from regular honey?
A: Subur is more than honey. It is raw honeycomb blended with nine other ingredients - nuts, spices and roots - each one named on the label, rather than being honey alone.

A: Subur is designed to be part of your daily routine. Consistent daily use is recommended.

A: Subur is made for both men and women. The blend includes ingredients valued for supporting overall wellbeing, vitality and fertility in both.
