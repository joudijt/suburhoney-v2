# About — SUBUR Honey

SUBUR Honey is a raw honeycomb blend of ten named ingredients, sold in Malaysia through Berkat Madinah Store. It is sold as a food eaten daily, not as a supplement and not as a medicine - a spoonful in the morning - and makes no medical claim of any kind. There are four ways to buy it: the 2 Berkat Madinah Store shops in Selangor (Ampang Jaya and Batu Caves), the store's own Shopee storefront and its TikTok Shop.
